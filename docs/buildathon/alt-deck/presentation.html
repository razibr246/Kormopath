Consumer-Grade Trust Architecture: Zero Friction & 100% Local Privacy

No Sign-Up Barrier · 100% On-Device Storage · Deterministic Math · Zod Guardrails.

Why Consumers Can Trust KormoPath:
✔ Zero Sign-Up Friction: Unlike enterprise software, consumers don't need accounts, passwords, or email verifications. Open any tool and get immediate value in under 30 seconds.

✔ 100% On-Device Privacy (Zero Leaks): All medical prescriptions, budget ledgers, and student CVs remain stored in browser localStorage. No centralized database exists to be breached or sold.

✔ Deterministic Math Invariant (lib/finops.ts): AI NEVER touches money. Payout calculations, MFS fees, and incentives are computed in pure TypeScript. Gross - Fees = Net holds down to the exact poisha.

✔ Verbatim Quote Evidence (lib/grounding.ts): The Auditor accepts skills ONLY if they literally appear in the user's CV. Unquoted or exaggerated claims are struck through and rejected.
Live Workspace Architecture: Verbatim quote checks, Web Worker code sandbox & open Telemetry trace.
Speaker Notes (45s)
To build a winning B2C product, the consumer must trust it implicitly. First, we remove all friction: no sign-up or passwords required. Second, privacy: consumer medical, financial, and student data is stored strictly on the local device, not our servers. Third, reliability: we treat LLMs as untrusted. AI never calculates financial math—pure deterministic code handles all poisha math. Skills must be grounded in exact verbatim quotes, and guardrails live inside Zod schemas to ensure zero hallucination.
