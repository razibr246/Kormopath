# KormoPath: AI you can check, for everyday life in Bangladesh

A young person in Bangladesh handles a dozen worries from one phone. A client doubts their CV. A fake bKash SMS asks for an OTP. A parent has chest pain at 2 a.m. A landlord threatens eviction. Today each worry means a different app, office or Facebook group, and generic AI chatbots answer with confident guesses: invented fees, doses, laws and dates.

KormoPath is a free, Bangla-first web app that brings 12 consumer tools onto one trust engine. Rokkha quotes the exact scam words in a message. MediScan sends danger signs to 999 before any AI call, then suggests the right kind of doctor and never gives a diagnosis. A hospital finder shows the nearest emergency care. Shohoj Budget tracks money to the poisha. Legal Aid explains the free government legal aid helpline (16699). MaathSathi plans a farmer's field. For freelancers, four Gemini agents take them from skill to taka: the Auditor keeps a skill only if its exact quote is in the CV, the Examiner tests real code and issues a signed passport, and fees are computed by code, never by AI. Buddy, an assistant inside every app, answers in Bangla voice or text with sourced numbers.

Every answer is checked before you see it, works offline with rule-based fallbacks, and keeps data on your device. Measured on the live site: 21/21 skills quoted word for word, 2/2 trap CVs caught, 0 invented dollar amounts, 640+ automated tests.

The business is B2C freemium: safety is free forever, and Pro is a proposed ৳99 per app or ৳299 for everything.
